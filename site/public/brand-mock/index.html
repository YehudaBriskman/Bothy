BothyBox: Brand options

The idea: the icon becomes two Bs for BothyBox, and the brand moment is a box that opens, two Bs that climb out, and stretch into the word. Every mark here is drawn by hand as geometric paths: no font is used, so there is no font licence to worry about.

A · Eight icon concepts

Each at 180, 32 and 16 px; the 16 px one also enlarged pixel for pixel, as a browser really draws it. Then on ink in one colour, on two skies, on a light tile, and as the favicon in a light and a dark browser tab.

B · The opening box, three ways

About 2 seconds: the box opens, the two Bs rise, pull the rest of the word out between them and settle into the lockup with a small overshoot. With reduced motion you see the finished lockup. Drag the scrubber to step through it by hand; the strip under each one is the same animation frozen at six moments.

Nav icon, idle

Hover or focus a mock nav brand: its lid gives a small wiggle, on a loop, while you stay.

C · The lockup

The recommended icon with the wordmark, side by side and stacked. Clear space is the dashed margin: keep it free of other marks and text. Below the minimum sizes, use the icon alone.

E · How it would be wired (later, not now)

  1. Favicon. Replace public/favicon.svg with the chosen icon's tile SVG (it already ships an SVG favicon, linked in prerender.tsx). Add a 32 px PNG fallback only if a browser we care about needs it.
  2. apple-touch-icon. A 180 × 180 PNG of the full-bleed variant (iOS rounds the corners itself), linked with <link rel="apple-touch-icon">. Generated from the SVG at build time and checked in, with a provenance line.
  3. Open Graph. The 1200 × 630 card puts the horizontal lockup on the night or dusk sky still, so the share image and the hero agree.
  4. Header and footer. The inline <Mark /> becomes the chosen icon (mono, currentColor); the text wordmark can stay live text or become the drawn wordmark with an accessible name.
  5. The loader, first visit only. Plays once over the hero, at most about 2 seconds, never blocking: the page is fully rendered and usable underneath, and any key, click, tap or scroll skips it.
  6. Never again that session. A sessionStorage flag written and read inside try/catch; if storage throws, it simply does not play.
  7. No layout shift. The loader draws in an overlay sized to the lockup's own box, in the place the lockup will be, and ends exactly as the static lockup.
  8. No JavaScript or reduced motion: the static lockup, already in the HTML. The animation only ever moves away from that state and back to it.
  9. Hover idle on the nav icon: CSS only, off for reduced motion, a few hundred bytes.

Hand-drawn geometric paths, written in concepts.js; no font, image or code from anywhere else. Every icon, the wordmark and both lockups are also plain files in svg/ (linked from each card). No formal trademark search has been done; see the design doc docs/plans/thin-bothy/brand-options.md.
