Three ideas for every section, and four new sections

Each section of the site as it is now, what is weak about it, and three designs with their motion. Every panel is live HTML using the site's own colours, glass and sky photographs. Words are from the site; anything invented is marked EXAMPLE. The recommended option carries a star.

  • Scrub stands in for scroll: drag it to see a scroll-linked motion at any point.
